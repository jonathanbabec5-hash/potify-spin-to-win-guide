# Spin to Win and Prize Verification marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Community event organizers and developers. **Offer.** Let a participant identify where to play and where to verify a code.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Publish a two-screen diagram after the organizers confirm the promotion terms and prize availability. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Review the public verification flow. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 2 event organizers review the verification guide. |

### Sales test

Validate one sponsor's interest after the organizer confirms fulfillment and cost. No sale or reward is recorded by a visit to a route. Assess interest in one sponsored event after funding, fulfillment, eligibility and terms are confirmed. Count sponsor revenue only on a reconciled invoice.

### Measurement and decision

Use `spin-to-win-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Original promotion logic and schema were inspected. No live draw, participant data, prize inventory, redemption or commercial availability was verified. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
